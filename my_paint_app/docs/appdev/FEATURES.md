# Features: JingyaoPaint (靖瑶的画板)

Flutter app, Android APK first; Chrome and Windows are used as debug targets.
Package `com.personal.jingyaopaint`, Dart package `jingyao_paint`, project folder `C:\AIProjects\my_paint_app`.

## v1.6.0 (2026-10-25): corner controls, move tool, round eraser
- **Undo/redo top right**: the undo bar now starts at the paper's top right for right-hand use; the tools launcher starts at the bottom right.
- **Draggable corner controls**: drag the undo bar or the "画笔 · 颜色" launcher anywhere and it glides (220 ms) to the nearest of the paper's four corners. The two never share a corner: dropping one on the other's corner picks the nearest free one. Corners are remembered (`undoCorner`, `toolsCorner`). A plain tap still works. When the launcher sits on the left, the drawer slides in from the left (arrow points right, swipe left to close); on the right, it slides in from the right. Code: `lib/widgets/corner_float.dart`. Verified by: the "Draggable corner controls" widget tests and on the tablet emulator.
- **Move tool (选择移动)**: a 10th tool. Switching to it selects the last stroke (dashed box with corner dots). Tap any stroke (lines, shapes, stamps, spray, text, mirrored strokes) to select it, and drag to move it. A whole drag undoes in one step; tapping empty paper clears the selection. Spray dots and stamps keep their look (same seed). Code: `Stroke.moved`, `Stroke.hits`, `DrawingController.hitStroke`. Verified by: the "Moving strokes" unit tests, the "Move tool and eraser options" widget tests, and on the tablet emulator.
- **Round eraser only**: the eraser no longer shows 颜料 or 形状 (and ignores a saved shape). Its panel says the tip is round and that long-pressing shows its size. Rainbow hides colors but keeps shapes; the move tool hides shapes and sliders.

## v1.5.0 (2026-10-25): tablets, zoom, tools drawer, new layout, small APKs
- **Tablet and landscape layouts**: on screens ≥600dp wide the tabs move to a side rail. The paper fills the space available but never shrinks below what's drawn, so rotating a tablet or opening a painting made on a bigger screen scales the whole sheet to fit instead of cropping it (FittedBox maps touches back to paper coordinates). The settings page is capped at 760dp wide and centered. Verified on a 2560×1600 tablet emulator in both orientations, and by widget tests at 1280×800, 800×1280, 851×393 and 600×960.
- **Pinch zoom (双指缩放)**: two fingers zoom (50%–800%) and pan. A stroke the first finger already started is dropped. A "150% · 适应屏幕" chip (top right) animates back to fit. Mouse-wheel zoom works in Chrome, and ⋮ → 放大 / 缩小 / 适应屏幕 covers anyone who can't pinch. Opening another painting resets the view. Verified by: the "Zoom" widget tests (pinch, limits, animated reset, drawing lands under the finger while zoomed) and on the emulator through the menu.
- **Tools drawer (画笔 · 颜色)**: brushes, colors, shapes, stamps and sliders live in a drawer that slides in from the right (dimmed backdrop; the buttons fade out). It opens from a bottom-right button that shows the current brush and color. It closes with ✕, a backdrop tap (which doesn't draw), Back, or a swipe on its header, and respects the system "remove animations" setting. Verified by: the "Tools drawer" widget tests, and on the emulator.
- **Top-bar layout**: new ⊕, save 💾 (saves now, with a confirmation message) and clear sit top right; undo/redo float at the paper's top left. The ⋮ menu unfolds downward under the app bar and scrolls, grouped as 分享 / 视图 / 画纸 / 画作 (删除这幅画 in red) / 设置. 设置 left the bottom bar, which keeps 画板 and 我的画作; it opens from ⋮ on both pages. Verified by: the "Top-right painting actions" tests, and on the emulator.
- **Small release APKs**: `tool\build_apk.ps1` now builds one obfuscated APK per CPU type: arm64 26.4 MB (most phones), armv7 24.2 MB, x86_64 27.9 MB (emulators). `-Universal` builds the single 64.8 MB APK.

## v1.4.0 (2026-10-24)
- **Send (发送给别人)**: from the canvas ⋮ menu or any gallery card. Choose 图片 PNG, 图片 JPG (smaller), PDF, or 画作文件 `.jypaint` (editable), then pick an app or person in the system share sheet. Exports render at 2× for print quality; JPG is encoded off the UI thread. In Chrome, if the browser can't share files, the file is downloaded instead. Code: `lib/share/`. Verified by: `test/export_test.dart` (real PNG/JPEG/PDF headers, painting round trip) and the widget test "send offers PNG, JPG, PDF and the editable painting file".
- **Receive and continue (打开继续画)**: on Android, opening a `.jypaint` from another app (chat, files) offers 靖瑶的画板. The painting is imported as a new painting (it never overwrites one) and opens on the canvas. In 我的画作, 导入画作文件 imports one with a file picker on every platform. Files that aren't paintings are rejected with a message. Code: `MainActivity.kt` + intent filters in `AndroidManifest.xml`, `lib/share/incoming_files.dart`, `import_actions.dart`, `PaintingSession.importPainting`. Verified by: the "Importing" tests. **The Android "open with" part is not verified yet** (no Android SDK to compile or run it).
- **Print (打印)**: canvas ⋮ menu or gallery card. Opens the system print dialog with the picture on one page, turned sideways for wide paintings. Code: `printPainting` in `share_actions.dart`.
- **Text tool (文字)**: the 9th tool. Tap the canvas to add text; tap text to edit or delete it; drag text to move it. The editor has 加粗, 斜体, 下划线, 删除线, 背景气泡, 左对齐/居中/右对齐, 字号 (12–120), 字体 (the cute fonts) and color. Opacity comes from the tool's slider. The last formatting is remembered. Code: `TextContent` and `Stroke.text` in `stroke.dart`, `lib/drawing/text_editor.dart`. Verified by: the "Text" group in `brush_test.dart` and widget tests for adding, editing, dragging, deleting and accessibility.
- **Better undo**: every edit is undoable now, including 清空画布, paper color, text edits, deletes and moves (a whole drag undoes in one step). Keeps the last 100 steps. Verified by: the "Undo history" tests.

## v1.3.0 (2026-10-24)
- **Shapes (形状)**: 直线, 箭头, 矩形, 圆形, 三角形, 星形 and 心形. Drag from one corner to the other; dragging backwards works too, and a tap draws nothing. Shapes use the current brush, color, size and opacity: outline brushes draw crisp geometry, while spray, rainbow and stamps follow the outline. **填充** (fill) works for closed shapes, and an eraser shape with fill clears its whole area. Saved as two corners and rebuilt exactly on load. Code: `ShapeKind` in `lib/drawing/stroke.dart`, shape row in `canvas_page.dart`. Verified by: the "Shapes" group in `test/brush_test.dart` (pixel tests) and widget tests "drawing a rectangle" and "fill only works for closed shapes".
- **Eraser range circle**: long-pressing (0.35 s) with the eraser shows a circle exactly as wide as the eraser. It follows the finger while erasing, is mirrored when mirror drawing is on, and hides on release. A mouse or stylus hovering with the eraser shows it too. It's drawn with a white and a dark ring so it's visible on any paper. Code: `CanvasOverlayPainter`. Verified by: widget tests "long-pressing with the eraser shows its range circle" and "a quick eraser stroke does not show the circle".
- **Cute fonts (字体)**: 快乐体 (ZCOOL KuaiLe, the default), 黄油体 (ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou), 手写体 (Long Cang) and 系统默认, picked in 设置 with sample text. Fonts work with any theme. All are SIL OFL; their licenses are bundled and shown under 设置 → 开源许可. They add about 15 MB in total (黄油体 alone is 8.3 MB). Code: `lib/theme/cute_font.dart`, `kFonts` in `app_theme.dart`. Verified by: widget test "the cute font is the default and can be changed" and the font files present in the web build.

## v1.2.0 (2026-10-24)
- **New brushes**: 喷枪 (spray dots), 彩虹笔 (color cycles along the line) and 印章 (stamps a heart, star or flower along the path, chosen with chips that appear when the stamp brush is selected). There are now 8 brushes. Spray dots and stamp wobble are seeded, so a saved painting reopens exactly the same. Code: `lib/drawing/stroke.dart`. Verified by: `test/brush_test.dart` ("look the same after saving and reloading", "every stamp shape draws").
- **Opacity slider (不透明度)**: each brush remembers its own opacity. Overlapping spray dots and stamps don't build up darker. At half opacity the eraser fades ink instead of removing it. Verified by: pixel tests in the "Opacity" group.
- **Mirror drawing (对称绘画)**: toggle in the ⋮ menu. Strokes are mirrored across the center, and a dashed guide line shows the axis (it isn't saved into the picture). Verified by: "a mirrored stroke also appears on the other side".
- **Paper color (画纸颜色)**: 6 soft papers (纯白, 奶油, 樱花, 天空, 薄荷, 薰衣草), saved with each painting and used as the default for new ones. Verified by: "paper color is saved with the painting and restored".
- **Collapsible tool panel**: the ⌄ button hides colors and sliders to give more room to draw.
- **Cute style**: round bubble brush buttons, a rounded sketchbook-style paper with a soft shadow, a rounded header and nav bar, a theme emoji in the title, and a friendlier empty gallery.
- **5 portable themes**: 樱花粉 (default), 薰衣草, 蜜桃奶昔, 薄荷糖 and 棉花糖, picked in 设置 with live preview cards. Each theme is a JSON template in `assets/themes/`. The module in `lib/theme/` (see its README) has no app dependencies and can be copied into another app.
- **Accessibility**: text and icon colors are derived from each theme by WCAG contrast math (`lib/theme/contrast.dart`), so pale backgrounds always get dark text. Every brush and color button has a screen-reader label and a tap area of at least 48×48. Verified by: `test/theme_test.dart` (WCAG AA on every theme) and widget tests that run Flutter's `textContrastGuideline`, `androidTapTargetGuideline` and `labeledTapTargetGuideline` on all 3 screens in all 5 themes. Also tested on a 360×640 phone at 1.3× text size in both languages.

## v1.1.0 (2026-10-24)
- **New name and header "靖瑶的画板"**, shown in the app bar, the Android launcher label and the web title. In English it reads "Jingyao's Drawing Board". Code: `lib/l10n.dart` (`appTitle`), `AndroidManifest.xml`. Verified by: widget test "opens in Chinese with the new title".
- **App icon**: a paint palette and brush on a violet→pink→peach gradient, including adaptive Android icons, web icons and the in-app logo. Regenerate with `tool/make_icons.ps1`. Verified by: viewing the generated PNGs.
- **New look**: gradient app bar, a colorful bottom navigation (画板 / 我的画作 / 设置) where each tab has its own color, and rounded cards. Code: `lib/app_theme.dart`, `lib/main.dart`.
- **Large, colorful brush buttons**: 钢笔 pen (pressure), 铅笔 pencil, 马克笔 marker, 荧光笔 highlighter (see-through) and 橡皮擦 eraser, each with its own color and label. Code: `lib/drawing/stroke.dart` (`BrushType`), `lib/drawing/canvas_page.dart` (`_BrushTile`).
- **Autosave and continue**: every finished edit is saved about 0.8 s later, and also when switching tabs or when the app goes to the background. On restart the app reopens the last painting. Code: `lib/painting_session.dart`. Verified by: model tests under "PaintingSession".
- **My Paintings gallery**: thumbnails with the date each painting was started and when it was last edited, an "正在画" badge on the open painting, tap to continue, menu to delete (asks first), and a "新建画作" button. Code: `lib/gallery/gallery_page.dart`. Verified by: widget tests for open, delete and autosave-to-gallery.
- **Year filter**: chips for 全部 and each year (with counts) filter the gallery by the year a painting was started. Verified by: widget test "the year filter shows only that year".
- **Remembered tools**: the selected brush, the color, a separate size for each brush, and up to 8 recently picked custom colors survive restarts. Code: `lib/settings.dart`. Verified by: model tests under "AppSettings".
- **Languages**: Simplified Chinese by default, plus English, switchable in 设置 and remembered. Material dialogs are localized too. Code: `lib/l10n.dart`, `lib/settings_page.dart`. Verified by: widget test "switching to English".
- **Storage**: one JSON file and one PNG thumbnail per painting, plus `index.json`, in the app's private folder. Writes are atomic (temp file then rename). In Chrome, browser localStorage is used instead (about 5 MB, for testing only). Code: `lib/gallery/`.

- **Freehand drawing** with pressure-sensitive strokes (`perfect_freehand`), using real stylus pressure or simulated pressure for fingers.
- **Palm rejection**: only the first finger or pen to touch the screen draws.
